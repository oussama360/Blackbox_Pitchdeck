My vision is to make industrial data collection and AI development accessible to every company, not just large enterprises. Today, collecting machine data often requires expensive hardware, custom integrations, and complex infrastructure, with many solutions costing hundreds of dollars per machine. This prevents many businesses from gathering the real\-world data needed to understand failures and train AI models.

I want to change that by reducing the cost of industrial data acquisition from around $500 per machine to less than $20. By creating a family of compact, plug\-and\-play industrial black boxes that can record signals, events, and operating conditions for years, companies will be able to monitor equipment, diagnose rare failures, and build AI datasets at a fraction of the current cost.

Long term, I want every machine to have a black box, just as every aircraft has one. The goal is to become the standard data layer for industrial AI, helping businesses of all sizes collect the data they need to improve reliability, reduce downtime, and develop smarter systems.

Most people still think this is a niche debugging problem. Before the rise of AI, only a small number of companies needed long\-term machine data logging, so the market remained limited. Today, however, every IoT and industrial AI company needs large amounts of real\-world machine data to train, validate, and improve their models.

What I believe most people get wrong is that they see data collection as a software or AI problem, when in reality it is a hardware accessibility problem. Companies cannot build AI without data, yet collecting that data often requires expensive and complex systems that can cost hundreds of dollars per machine.

I have not seen another company offering a simple, ultra\-low\-cost solution focused on this gap. As AI adoption accelerates, the demand for machine data is growing rapidly, but affordable tools to collect that data from existing equipment are still largely unavailable. I believe reducing the cost of data acquisition from around $500 to less than $20 unlocks an entirely new market and makes industrial AI accessible to far more companies.
